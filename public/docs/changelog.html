    Changelog

    2026-11-03 — first release

    Twenty-six native mods at 1.0.0: fps-display, sprint-indicator, key-overlay, cps-counter, info-hud, custom-hud, zoomify, simple-zoom, waypoints and, reading game state, armor-hud, potion-counter, totem-counter, effect-timer, apple-skin, pick-up-notifier and, hooking Horizon's camera and input, freelook, freecam, snappy-tappy, better-hurt-cam and, in chat and tooltips, more-chat-history, chat-notify, chat-animation and durability-tooltip, and in crosshair and tooltip drawing crosshair-indicator, entity-crosshair and shulker-box-tooltip, each tested (unit tests), formatted, clippy-clean and applied on Horizon ba6344a with horizon-mixins d9c83d7. The catalogue covers the 186 mods in the Tempest cross-pack matrix.
