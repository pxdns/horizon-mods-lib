    DMCA and takedowns

    This library re-implements the behaviour of other people's mods. If you think a project here copies your work, or that something else in this repository infringes a right you hold, you can have it looked at and, if you are right, removed.

    How to send a notice

    Open an issue at pxdns/horizon-mods-lib titled DMCA notice, or, if you prefer not to post publicly, message the repository owner pxdns through their GitHub profile and ask for a private channel. GitHub also takes notices directly under its own DMCA policy, which applies to this repository whatever is done here.

    A notice should have the elements the law asks for (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)):

    1. Your name and a way to contact you.
    2. The work you say is infringed, with a link to it.
    3. The material here you say infringes, with the file path or URL (a line range helps).
    4. A statement that you believe in good faith that the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law.
    5. A statement that the information in the notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
    6. Your physical or electronic signature.

    What happens next

    The material is reviewed within a few days. If it is infringing, or if there is real doubt, it is removed from main and from the site, the release asset is deleted, and the project's page says so. Where the complaint is that a port is too close to the original, the fix may be a rewrite rather than a removal; you will be told which.

    Counter-notice

    If something was taken down and you believe that was a mistake, send a counter-notice by the same route with your name, contact details, the material removed and where it was, a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe in good faith it was removed by mistake or misidentification, and your consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for your address (or, outside the US, any judicial district in which GitHub may be found), and your signature. Material may be restored after the period the law allows, unless the sender tells us they have gone to court.

    Repeat infringers

    Contributors who repeatedly submit infringing material lose the right to contribute.

    This page describes how the project handles notices. It is not legal advice.
