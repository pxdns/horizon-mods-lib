    The library

    26 mods, one project each.

    Every card is a folder in mods/ with its own logic, tests and release. Click through for what it does, its settings and who it is ported from.

    FPS Display

    Frames per second in a corner of the screen, coloured by how smooth they are.

    fps_display 1.0.0 · 4 tests

    Sprint Indicator

    Shows Sprinting, Walking or Sneaking in a corner of the screen while you move.

    sprint_indicator 1.0.0 · 4 tests

    Key Overlay

    Shows W, A, S, D, jump and the mouse buttons on screen and lights the ones you hold.

    key_overlay 1.0.0 · 5 tests

    CPS Counter

    Your clicks per second in a corner of the screen.

    cps_counter 1.0.0 · 3 tests

    Info HUD

    Your speed, the way you face and your dimension in a corner of the screen, in the spirit of MiniHUD's info lines.

    info_hud 1.0.0 · 6 tests

    Custom HUD

    One box with the lines you pick: FPS, coordinates, facing and clicks per second, on the side you choose.

    custom_hud 1.0.0 · 4 tests

    Zoomify

    Hold a key to zoom in, turn the wheel to zoom further, with smooth easing and slower turning.

    zoomify 1.0.0 · 7 tests

    Simple Zoom

    Press a key to zoom in and press it again to zoom out; the wheel changes the zoom meanwhile.

    simple_zoom 1.0.0 · 7 tests

    Waypoints

    Name places with /wp, list them, and track one: its distance and direction show on screen.

    waypoints 1.0.0 · 8 tests

    Armor HUD

    Your armor pieces and how much durability each has left, in a corner of the screen.

    armor_hud 1.0.0 · 5 tests

    Potion Counter

    How many potions, splash potions and lingering potions you carry.

    potion_counter 1.0.0 · 4 tests

    Totem Counter

    How many totems of undying you carry, red when you have none.

    totem_counter 1.0.0 · 4 tests

    Effect Timer

    Your active potion effects and how long each has left, red in the last ten seconds.

    effect_timer 1.0.0 · 6 tests

    Apple Skin

    Your hunger and saturation as numbers, and what the food in your hand would restore.

    apple_skin 1.0.0 · 4 tests

    Pick Up Notifier

    +3 Cobblestone: a short list of what you just picked up (and, if you like, dropped).

    pick_up_notifier 1.0.0 · 7 tests

    Freelook

    Hold a key and move the mouse to look around without turning your player.

    freelook 1.0.0 · 7 tests

    Freecam

    Detach the camera and fly it around while your player stands still.

    freecam 1.0.0 · 7 tests

    Snappy Tappy

    When you hold two opposite movement keys, the one pressed last wins, so you switch direction at once.

    snappy_tappy 1.0.0 · 4 tests

    Better Hurt Cam

    Tone the camera shake down when you are hit, and switch the death tilt off.

    better_hurt_cam 1.0.0 · 4 tests

    More Chat History

    Chat remembers up to 2000 lines instead of 100, so you can scroll back through a long conversation.

    more_chat_history 1.0.0 · 3 tests

    Chat Notify

    Plays a sound when someone says your name in chat.

    chat_notify 1.0.0 · 5 tests

    Chat Animation

    New chat lines slide up into place instead of appearing at once.

    chat_animation 1.0.0 · 4 tests

    Durability Tooltip

    Adds a Durability line to the tooltip of tools, weapons and armor.

    durability_tooltip 1.0.0 · 4 tests

    Crosshair Indicator

    Turns your crosshair red when it is over a living creature.

    crosshair_indicator 1.0.0 · 5 tests

    Entity Crosshair

    Replaces the crosshair with a style and colour of your choice, red over living entities.

    entity_crosshair 1.0.0 · 5 tests

    Shulker Box Tooltip

    Shows what is inside a shulker box in its tooltip: totals by item, and a slot grid.

    shulker_box_tooltip 1.0.0 · 6 tests
