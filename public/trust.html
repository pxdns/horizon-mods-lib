    Trust

    Built so that you do not have to take our word.

    A native mod runs with your rights. Here is everything this library does about that, and what it asks of you.

    Source, not binaries

    A .hnmod holds Rust and TOML. Read it before you build it; the launcher validates it without running anything.

    Pinned and reproducible

    Exact Horizon and horizon-mixins commits in every project. The package is deterministic: same commit, same SHA-256, published beside the file.

    Clean-room ports

    Written from behaviour. No upstream code, art or text, and the credits name every original and its licence.

    Icons, credited

    Catalogue icons are each mod's own, from Modrinth, named to their authors on every page and gone on request. The nine projects have their own drawn icons.

    Nothing phones home

    No network access in any mod, no dependencies, no analytics on this site, no third-party fonts or scripts.

    A way to say stop

    If something here is yours and should not be, the DMCA page says how to ask, and what happens next.

    The honest limits. This is an unofficial project, not endorsed by the Horizon project, Mojang or Microsoft. Native mods are experimental in Horizon. The mods are unit-tested and type-checked against Horizon in CI, and have not been played through a real window by CI. Read the security notes and the disclaimer.
