`simple-zoom`, `waypoints` and, reading game state, `armor-hud`, `potion-counter`, `totem-counter`, `effect-timer`, `apple-skin`, `pick-up-notifier` and, hooking Horizon's camera and input, `freelook`, `freecam`, `snappy-tappy` and `better-hurt-cam`, each tested (unit tests), formatted, clippy-clean and applied on Horizon `ba6344a`
