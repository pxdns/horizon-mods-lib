# Changelog

## 2026-11-03 — first release

Thirty-two native mods at 1.0.0: `fps-display`, `sprint-indicator`, `key-overlay`, `cps-counter`, `info-hud`, `custom-hud`, `zoomify`,
`simple-zoom`, `waypoints` and, reading game state, `armor-hud`, `potion-counter`, `totem-counter`, `effect-timer`, `apple-skin`, `pick-up-notifier` and, hooking Horizon's camera and input, `freelook`, `freecam`, `snappy-tappy`, `better-hurt-cam` and, in chat and tooltips, `more-chat-history`, `chat-notify`, `chat-animation` and `durability-tooltip`, and in crosshair and tooltip drawing `crosshair-indicator`, `entity-crosshair` and `shulker-box-tooltip`, `combat-hitboxes` (a wrapped ray-versus-box test), `ping-view` (the connection ping, from a hook on its logger), `invmove` (walk with the inventory open), `better-mount-hud` (health, speed and jump of your mount), `minimap-plus` (corner, size, zoom keys and a big map for Horizon's minimap) and `password-hider` (masks the password in /login and /register), each tested (unit tests), formatted, clippy-clean and applied on Horizon `ba6344a`
with horizon-mixins `d9c83d7`. The catalogue covers the 186 mods in the Tempest cross-pack matrix.
