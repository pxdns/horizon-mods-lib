# Copyright and takedown policy

*(Draft for the site. Have a lawyer read it before you publish it. Replace the bracketed parts.)*

[Project name] respects other people's copyright and expects its users to do the same. We respond to notices of
claimed infringement and, where the law applies, follow the notice-and-takedown process of the US Digital
Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512).

## Sending a notice

Send it to our designated agent:

> [Name or role], [postal address], [email address], [phone]

A notice must include, in writing:

1. Your physical or electronic signature (or your authorised agent's).
2. The copyrighted work you say is infringed (or a list, if several works are covered by one notice).
3. The material you say infringes, and where it is, with enough detail to find it (the exact URL or file path).
4. Your contact details: address, phone and email.
5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the owner, the owner's agent or
   the law.
6. A statement, **under penalty of perjury**, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or are
   authorised to act for the owner.

A notice that is missing these may not be acted on. Knowingly sending a false notice can make you liable for
damages (§ 512(f)).

## What we do

- We review a complete notice promptly and may remove or disable access to the material.
- We tell the person responsible for the material (if we can) and give them a copy of the notice.
- We keep a record of each notice (see `templates/incident-log.md`).
- We end the access of people who are repeat infringers, in appropriate circumstances.

## If your material was removed: counter-notice

If you believe the removal was a mistake or the material is authorised, send our agent a written counter-notice with:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. The material removed and where it was before removal.
3. A statement, **under penalty of perjury**, that you have a good-faith belief it was removed by mistake or
   misidentification.
4. Your name, address and phone, and a statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the federal court for
   your address (or, if outside the US, for [our district]), and that you will accept service from the person who
   sent the notice.

We pass the counter-notice to the complainant. Unless they tell us they have started a court action, we may restore
the material not less than 10 and not more than 14 business days later (§ 512(g)).

## Mojang, Microsoft and other game rights holders

We do not host Minecraft's code or assets. If you believe something we host does contain them, send a notice as
above, or write to [contact] and say where it is. We will look at it the same day we see it.

## Mod authors and artists

If we show your mod's name, description or icon and you want it changed or removed, write to [contact] with the page
and what you want. We will do it without needing a formal notice.
