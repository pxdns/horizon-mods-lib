# A counter-notice arrived

1. Check it has the four elements in DMCA-POLICY.md (signature, the material and its former location, the perjury
   statement, name/address/consent to jurisdiction). If not, ask for what's missing.
2. Send a copy to the original complainant, with the date, and tell them we may restore the material not less than
   10 and not more than 14 business days after we received the counter-notice, unless they notify us they have
   filed an action seeking a court order against the user.
3. Write the dates in `incident-log.md`: received, forwarded, earliest restore, latest restore.
4. If the complainant says they filed suit before the restore date, **do not restore**.
5. If they stay silent, restore within the window (on the latest day if you are unsure) and note it.
6. Never restore Mojang/Microsoft assets, whatever the counter-notice says: our own rule in ASSETS.md is that we don't
   host them. Ask a lawyer.
