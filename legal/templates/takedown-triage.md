# A notice arrived: first hour

Do not reply yet. Do not delete evidence.

1. **Save it.** Keep the original message with headers. Open an entry in `incident-log.md`.
2. **Who is it from?** The rights holder, an agent (law firm, enforcement company) or a host (GitHub, Vercel,
   Cloudflare) relaying it. If it came through a host, read the host's own process: it has its own deadline.
3. **What is claimed?** Name the work and the exact URLs/files. Is it an asset (texture, sound), code, an icon,
   the name/logo, or a mod's description?
4. **Is it complete?** Check the six elements in DMCA-POLICY.md. If something is missing, you may ask for it; you
   don't have to act on an incomplete notice, but ask a lawyer before ignoring one from a host.
5. **Is it true?** Search the repo and build for the named material (see ASSETS.md). If we do host it, take it
   down now and decide the rest afterwards. If it is ours or authorised, keep the evidence of that.
6. **Tell the owner.** Whoever put the material up gets the notice and a deadline to answer.
7. **Get a lawyer** for anything beyond an obvious asset removal: a claim on code, a threat of suit, a claim from
   Mojang/Microsoft, or a counter-notice.

Deadlines: a host's deadline can be hours. Write it in the log.
